CLARION: Continuous Latent Augmented-Retrieval Inference On N-cores

A parallel CPU reimplementation of the CLaRa continuous RAG architecture, designed to eliminate computational bottlenecks in embedding generation and cosine similarity search via a hybrid NumPy + Cython and OpenMP approach.

Authors: Avner EL BAZ, Paco GOZE

Fundamental Architecture & Fused Kernel

The optimized backend reorganizes tensors into C-contiguous format to leverage the compiler's SIMD vectorization. Attention is performed in continuous (streaming) mode by blocks to avoid global allocation:

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax\(\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V\)

To propagate gradients through discrete Top-K routing, the pipeline implements a Straight-Through Estimator (STE) with a Softmax relaxation during backpropagation:

\(\nabla s_{b}=\frac{1}{\tau}p_{b}\odot(\nabla y_{b}-\langle\nabla y_{b},p_{b}\rangle)\)
